Four incredible parts of our universe.

🌌

Galaxies

Huge collections of stars, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity.

🪐

Planets

Worlds ranging from rocky planets like Earth to enormous gas giants like Jupiter.

⭐

Stars

Massive balls of plasma that produce light and energy through nuclear fusion.

🕳️

Black Holes

Regions of space with gravity so strong that even light cannot escape once it crosses the event horizon.

Why Explore?

Human curiosity has always pushed us beyond what we already know.

From the first observations of the night sky to modern space telescopes, every generation has found new questions to ask. Studying space helps us understand where our planet came from, how stars and planets form, and whether other worlds could exist beyond our own.

The universe is bigger than you think.

Keep looking up. There is always something new to discover.
